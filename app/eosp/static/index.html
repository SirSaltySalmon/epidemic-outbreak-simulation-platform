How this simulation works

This page shows recorded outbreak lines alongside a computer simulation of how infection could spread among a small, carefully defined group of people tied to major airports—not the whole global population, and not everyone still in the original travel context.

The colored bands on the chart are uncertainty: the model is run many times with slightly different random outcomes so you can see a plausible range, not a single guess.

  • The map shows scenario risk from the hub-timeline ensemble: orange heat and green rings can show either stock (people attributed to the hub across compartments) or pressure (infectious-weighted presence) when replay is available. It is not a census of everyone at each city.
  • Scenarios (sidebar) change assumptions—such as restricting travel, or implementing social distancing lowering transmission rates, to compare what may happen.
  • Limitations: mean contacts per day is fixed; lack of official to infer from; the model cannot predict real-world policy or behavior changes

Technical details

Cohort and line list. The hub-timeline engine ingests individual CaseRecord rows (and cohort rows that spawn synthetic agents at onset) with valid IATA hubs from OpenFlights-derived route data. First recorded case is skip, as patient stayed on cruise ship. (see EOSP_HUB_INDEX_CASE_ID).

Mobility. Synthetic agents move between hubs using frequency-weighted routes from routes.dat; home-airport sampling follows the design spec (mix of current hub vs route-weighted destinations). Observed individuals follow fixed pinned hub rules from symptom onset through hospitalisation where recorded.

Dynamics. Forward simulation uses daily negative-binomial contact counts, load-dependent transmission, scenario-tunable compartment progression (E→P→I→H/R/D for synthetics; observed rows follow recorded timeline per spec). This replaces the retired vectorised ABM (see docs/ABM_RETIREMENT.md).

Inference vs. simulation. Bayesian posteriors supply summary parameters (e.g. transmissibility, contact intensity, incubation, severity). The hub-timeline kernel draws per-trajectory values and runs independently of the MCMC chain.

Scenarios. The catalog’s hub_timeline blocks and optional parameter_overrides / network_modifications feed eosp.services.hub_timeline; dashboards read cached forecasts produced after a researcher or batch run.

Provenance. Flight baseline snapshots hash canonical JSON and may incorporate a SHA-256 of the routes file. OpenFlights derivatives are ODbL—see project README for attribution when publishing graphs or downstream datasets.
